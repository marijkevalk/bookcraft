# Quality rubric — steamy romance

The checklist the analysis evaluates a manuscript against. Extensible: it grows
over time, and the learning overview sharpens it from real review outcomes.
Every flagged issue must carry a **verbatim quote + location** — never a vague claim.

## 1. Language / line-level
- Spelling, grammar, punctuation errors; typos / autocorrect artifacts
- Crutch / filler words and repetition (names, "just", body parts in spice scenes)
- Filter words ("she felt", "she saw") that weaken immediacy
- POV and tense slips

## 2. Prose & voice
- Show-vs-tell balance; sensory detail
- Dialogue quality & naturalness; over-used or clumsy dialogue tags
- Sentence rhythm & variety (monotone if all the same length)
- Cliché / purple prose
- Consistent pen-name voice

## 3. Structure & pacing
- Chapter-1 hook (drives Amazon "Look Inside" conversion)
- Pacing: slow opening, saggy middle, rushed ending
- Chapter balance & cliffhangers
- Satisfying HEA/HFN ending (genre requirement — a weak/missing happy end tanks reviews)

## 4. Characters & relationship arc
- Likability / agency; consistent behaviour
- Distinct hero/heroine voices
- Believable relationship build (watch for insta-love)
- Chemistry & tension build-up

## 5. Genre & market fit (steamy romance)
- Heat level & spice pacing vs. what the cover/blurb/category promise
- Trope execution (enemies-to-lovers, mafia, …): is the promise delivered?
- Repetition / sameness across spice scenes
- Consent & dynamics handled sensitively
- Length appropriate for category / price

## 6. Consistency & continuity
- Names, physical details, ages, backstory, timeline
- Series continuity; loose threads / plot holes

## Severity
- **high** — likely to cause bad reviews or returns
- **medium** — noticeably hurts quality
- **low** — polish
